Privacy Policy

Last updated: [RELEASE DATE]. The Turkish version is the governing text.

In short: we never use your data for ads and never sell it. AI only runs on the photo, text or message you send, and only with your consent. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

1. Who we are

[NAME / COMPANY], [ADDRESS]. Contact: [EMAIL].

2. Who the app is for

Hally is for adults (parents and other adults in a family). Children cannot create accounts. Information about children is entered by their parent. A parent can optionally put a child's own phone in child mode: it is paired with a one-time code from the parent's phone, no account, email or password is created, and the child sees only their own jobs, cherries and rewards. Once a day the child may answer "How are you today?" with a face and optional reason; only parents (not caregivers) see these answers, they are not a medical assessment and are deleted after 90 days. With "Ask for something" the child can send a request to the parents (for example "Can I go to my friend's? · Zeynep"); the parents' yes/no and optional note go back to the child. Requests are seen only by parents and deleted after 90 days. Parents can optionally set a pocket-money value for cherries and a weekly allowance; the child can set a savings goal and ask to convert cherries. Hally never holds or moves money; it only records that a parent said they paid.

3. Data we process

  • Account: email, name, Google or Apple account id, language.
  • Family: family name, time zone, members and roles, invitations.
  • Children: first name, optional birth year, colour, optional profile photo.
  • Child trackers you choose to use: sleep, feeding, medication and doses, vaccines, height and weight, health appointments, homework and reading time, exam dates and exam results (parents only), weekly school timetable and school-bag checklist, screen time, habits, cherry points, school-run plan. Some of this is health data.
  • Child mode (optional, on the child's own phone): a device token (stored as a hash), the device name and when it was last seen; jobs and screen-free ideas the child marks done and the parent's approval; optionally the city a parent picks for the weather (a city point rounded to about 10 km, never a home address); the child's total screen time per day as measured by the phone. On Android this uses Usage access only to add up today's screen time; which apps were used is not sent to us.
  • Plans: calendar, tasks, shopping list, expenses.
  • Calendar sync (optional): for phone calendars you share with "My family": each event's title (or just "busy"), time, calendar name and colour, so the other adults in your family see them in Hally; a hash of your private computer calendar link and when it was last used. Calendars you keep to "Only me" stay on your phone and are never sent to us. Hally asks for calendar permission and writes its own events into a separate "Hally" calendar on your phone. Shared events are deleted when you disconnect, leave the family or delete your account.
  • Photos and text you send to Hally: to suggest tasks and events. Originals are stored encrypted and deleted after 30 days.
  • Assistant messages and family chat (chat photos are stored encrypted).
  • Device: push token and platform, to send reminders.
  • Usage metrics without names, emails or text; crash reports (if enabled) without identity, content or screenshots.

We do not collect location, contacts, microphone audio or advertising identifiers.

4. AI processing

Photo reading and "Ask Hally" send the content you provide to an AI service ([AI PROVIDER]). The app asks for your consent before the first use; you can withdraw it under Profile → Privacy & account. The content is used only to answer your request. Hally never adds anything to your calendar or tasks without your approval.

Voice ("Speak to Hally"): the microphone listens only while you use the mic button, never in the background. Your phone's own speech service (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone) turns your speech into text. We never receive or store the audio; only the resulting text goes to "Ask Hally", like a typed message.

Spoken day summary ("What's on today?"): our server builds it only from the family's own calendar, tasks, school run and medicine reminders; it is never sent to an AI provider. Your phone's own voice reads it aloud. If you turn on the morning notification, we store only the time you chose.

5. Who we share with

Other adults in your family; people you send a helper link to (they see only what you shared); service providers acting on our behalf: hosting ([HOSTING PROVIDER, COUNTRY]), the AI service, Open-Meteo for the weather (only the approximate location of the city the family picked, for screen-free ideas, optional), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service, Google and Apple sign-in, Sentry for crash reports (if enabled); and authorities where the law requires. We do not sell data or share it with ad networks. Some providers are outside Türkiye and the EU; such transfers rely on your consent or standard contractual clauses.

6. Security and retention

Connections use HTTPS; stored photos are AES-encrypted; sign-in tokens stay in the phone's secure storage. Data is kept until you delete your account or leave the family; original capture photos for 30 days; a child's daily mood answers and requests for 90 days; caregivers' end-of-day reports (summary, incidents, note, optional photo) until the account or family is deleted; backups for at most [BACKUP DAYS] days.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, export, delete and object (KVKK art. 11, GDPR). In the app: Download my data and Delete my account under Profile → Privacy & account (details). For anything else email [EMAIL]; we reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your data protection authority.
